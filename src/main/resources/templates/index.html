The Explainable 6-Factor ATS Formula

We believe candidates deserve transparency. Our core ATS score uses a deterministic mathematical equation:

ATS Score = (S_keywords × 0.30) + (S_experience × 0.20) + (S_format × 0.20) + (S_contact × 0.10) + (S_summary × 0.10) + (S_education × 0.10)

Total Score: 0 – 100 Points • Enforced strictly in Java (ResumeAnalyzerService.java)

Keyword Match 30%

Regex word boundaries match skills without false substring collisions (e.g. Java vs JavaScript).

Work Experience 20%

Evaluates employment duration, bullet density, and power action verbs.

Formatting Density 20%

Validates length (450–1,200 words), standard headers, and absence of corrupted tokens.

Contact Completeness 10%

Verifies email, telephone, LinkedIn, and GitHub/Portfolio URL presence.

Summary Statement 10%

Verifies professional summary or objective outlining specialization.

Education Verification 10%

Detects recognized academic degrees (B.Tech, B.S., M.S., University).
