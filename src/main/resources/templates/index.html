The Real Problem

Why Career Preparation is Broken Today

Students and professionals often have to move between many different websites to understand career options, build a resume, check ATS compatibility, identify missing skills, find learning resources, discover genuine jobs, prepare for interviews and track applications.

Context Switching & Confusion

Moving between separate websites for resumes, jobs, courses, and interviews creates confusion and makes it difficult to decide what to do next.

Opaque ATS Black Boxes

Conventional tools return an arbitrary number without explaining why points were lost, which skills were missing, or how to provide concrete project evidence.

Dead-End Skill Gaps

Discovering a missing qualification leaves candidates stranded without recommended tutorials, practical project blueprints, or realistic study schedules.

Anti-Hallucination Mathematics

The Explainable 6-Factor ATS Formula

We believe candidates deserve transparency. Our core ATS score uses a deterministic mathematical equation implemented in Java (ResumeAnalyzerService.java):

ATS Score = (S_keywords × 0.30) + (S_experience × 0.20) + (S_format × 0.20) + (S_contact × 0.10) + (S_summary × 0.10) + (S_education × 0.10)

Total Score: 0 – 100 Points • Enforced strictly in Java • Zero stochastic hallucination

Keyword Match (S_keywords) 30%

Regex word boundaries match skills without false substring collisions (e.g. Java vs JavaScript).

Work Experience (S_experience) 20%

Evaluates employment duration, bullet density, and Google X-Y-Z active achievement verbs.

Formatting Density (S_format) 20%

Validates length (450–1,200 words), standard headers, and absence of corrupted Unicode tokens.

Contact Completeness (S_contact) 10%

Verifies email, telephone, LinkedIn, and GitHub/Portfolio URL presence.

Summary Statement (S_summary) 10%

Verifies targeted professional summary outlining domain specialization.

Education Verification (S_education) 10%

Detects recognized academic degrees (B.Tech, B.S., M.S., University) and graduation year.

Security & Ethics

Privacy & Responsible AI Safeguards

We report only the privacy and security practices actually implemented in our codebase. No unverified certifications or exaggerated compliance claims.

In-Memory PII Redaction

Before any resume text is evaluated by external AI models, our regex pipeline automatically redacts candidate mobile phone numbers and email addresses into safe placeholders.

BCrypt Password Protection

User passwords are hashed using Spring Security's BCryptPasswordEncoder (work factor 10). Passwords are never logged or stored in plaintext.

No Hiring Guarantees

We explicitly disclaim unsupported promises regarding guaranteed placements or hiring probabilities. The platform is an educational diagnostic and career acceleration tool.
